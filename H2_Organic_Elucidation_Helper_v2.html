Enter a reagent, condition, or part of a reaction. Results show the possible transformation, reaction type and functional group(s) reacting.

How to use it for elucidation

This tool is designed as a deduction aid rather than an automatic structure generator.

  1. Put the exact observation into box ①. Read the possible functional groups and reaction type.
  2. Use other observations in the question to eliminate possibilities.
  3. Put a reagent + conditions into box ② when you want to work forwards from a functional group.
  4. For quantitative questions, check how many moles of reagent/gas/product are involved; the supplied notes specifically emphasise using this to deduce the number of functional groups.
  5. When product structures or molecular formulae are supplied, work backwards and compare carbon numbers. The notes highlight step-up reactions such as KCN/HCN and step-down reactions such as oxidative cleavage, side-chain oxidation and iodoform reactions.
Important: “possible” means the observation can support more than one deduction. Do not identify a unique structure from one test alone unless the other evidence rules out alternatives.
